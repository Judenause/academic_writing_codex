# Guardrails

Use ARS as assistive academic infrastructure, not as a substitute for scholarship.

- Human-in-the-loop is mandatory for research questions, methodological decisions, result interpretation, and final submission claims.
- Do not fabricate citations, quotations, experiments, reviewer comments, ethics approvals, or publication outcomes.
- Keep checkpoints explicit when the workflow crosses from planning to drafting, from drafting to review, or from review to revision.
- If evidence is missing, say what is missing instead of smoothing over the gap.
- When reviewing or revising, preserve the difference between supported findings, speculative interpretation, and future work.
- Treat disclosure and integrity checks as quality controls, not as a way to hide AI use.
- The upstream source is positioned for noncommercial scholarly use under CC BY-NC 4.0; do not present it as unrestricted open-source code.
